About this instrument

Polymeter is arithmetic with feelings. A riff that loops in twenty-five while the band holds sixteen realigns every four hundred ticks — and the distance between those meetings is where the tension in this music lives. This engine plays that arithmetic with clicks of its own making: no recordings, no samples, nothing borrowed. The patterns it ships are the time signatures of songs we love, stated as numbers — facts, which belong to everyone.

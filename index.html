Craft & recognition

Leadership never cured the urge to build.

At Electrolux I led interaction design across the connected portfolio — setting the Smart Home UX direction and building universal interaction models that held across product categories and brands. The goal was always to make complexity invisible; that work became four filed patents. But the urge never fully left: today I run a small studio, designing and shipping iOS apps end to end.

Invisible

One UX logic scaling across hardware, software, and operating systems — regardless of category or brand. Complexity, made to disappear.

Patents

Four filed patents from the Electrolux work: a proximity-aware adaptive display, user recognition with personalised preferences, a safety mode that detects unauthorised users, and an appliance interface design.
